# V1-M19 — Stylus support (optional)

**Tag at the end:** `v0.19.0`. **Target:** `v1-optional` (drop first if budget is short). **Model:** mid-tier, medium effort. **Prerequisite:** M17 done (M18 is independent).

**Requirements:** STY-001, STY-002, STY-003, STY-004.

Goal: once a stylus has been seen, the pen draws and fingers navigate (zoom, pan, tap zones, swipes) and never draw; palm touches are ignored while the pen is near; a setting lets fingers draw again. Without a stylus nothing changes (M14 behavior). The decision logic already exists: `pointerPolicy` and its fixtures `PP09…PP16` (M13). This milestone feeds it real values and adds palm rejection.

## Core part (first)

Copy `docs/contracts/core/palm.ts` + `fixtures/palm.cases.ts` into `src/app/core/`; write the spec (red); implement `reducePen` (`pen-down`, `pen-move`, `pen-hover` → `active: true`; `pen-up`, `pen-leave`, `pen-cancel` → `active: false`; always `lastT = t`) and `shouldIgnoreTouch` (`active || now - lastT <= graceMs`, inclusive).

## Steps (commit after each)

### S1 — Stylus state and detection (STY-001, STY-003)

- `StylusStore` (`state/`): `stylusSeen` (persisted in the preferences store, key `stylus.seen`, default false), `allowFingerDrawing` (key `stylus.allowFinger`, default `T:stylus.allowFingerDrawingDefault`), `pen: Signal<PenState>` (not persisted; updated through a small non-reactive handle for speed, mirrored to the signal at most once per animation frame).
- In `input/PointerInput` (the only place that reads `pointerType`, ARC-006): any pointer event with `pointerType === 'pen'` sets `stylusSeen` (persist once) and feeds the pen state: `pointerdown` → `pen-down`, `pointermove` with a button pressed → `pen-move`, `pointermove` without buttons (hover) → `pen-hover`, `pointerup` → `pen-up`, `pointerleave`/`pointerout` → `pen-leave`, `pointercancel` → `pen-cancel`; `t = event.timeStamp`.

### S2 — Policy inputs and palm rejection (STY-002, STY-004)

- The `PointerContext` built on every `pointerdown` now carries the real `stylusSeen` and `allowFingerDrawing` (until now always `false`). No other file changes the decision (the grep test of M13 keeps guarding this).
- Palm rejection in `PointerInput`: when `stylusSeen` and `pointerType === 'touch'` and `shouldIgnoreTouch(pen, event.timeStamp, T:stylus.palmRejectionGraceMs)`: ignore the pointer completely (no track, no gesture, no drawing, no zoom). A touch already in progress when the pen arrives is cancelled if it has not moved more than the tap threshold.
- Resulting behavior (verify against the fixtures `PP09…PP16`): with a tool active and `stylusSeen`, a **pen** draws; a **touch** gives role `gestures` (pan when zoomed, tap zones, swipes at fit zoom); a **mouse** still draws; with `allowFingerDrawing` a touch draws again. Two touches still pinch.

### S3 — Setting in the tool popup (STY-003)

- In `ToolPopup` of the pen and the highlighter add one toggle button with icon `T:icons.fingerDrawing` (toggled look when on; tooltip/label from translation keys `tooltip.fingerDrawing` and `a11y.fingerDrawing`); it flips `allowFingerDrawing` (persisted). Show it always (also before a stylus was seen) so a user can switch it ahead of time.

## Tests

- Unit: `palm.spec.ts` from fixtures; `StylusStore` persistence and defaults; `PointerInput` with synthetic events: first pen event sets `stylusSeen`; pen events produce the expected `PenState`; touch ignored within the grace window and accepted after; policy inputs reflect the store.
- Existing `gestures` fixtures `PP09…PP16` must stay green (no change to `pointerPolicy`).
- E2E (Chromium and WebKit, synthetic events created in `page.evaluate` with `new PointerEvent('pointerdown', { pointerType: 'pen', … })` and `pointerType: 'touch'` dispatched on the stage): before any pen event a touch drag with the pen tool draws; after a pen event, the pen draws and a touch drag does not (a tap zone tap turns the slide, a swipe turns it at fit zoom, a pinch zooms); a touch within 300 ms after pen-up is ignored; after turning on "allow finger drawing" a touch draws again; the flag and the toggle survive a reload.
- Manual (owner, on a tablet with a stylus; add to `docs/device-checklist.md`): the pen draws with hover/palm resting on the screen without stray marks; finger swipes turn slides while the pen tool is active; "allow finger drawing" works; note the device and stylus.

## Do not

- Do not change `pointerPolicy`, its fixtures or other layers' decision logic; do not add pressure sensitivity, tilt or eraser-end handling (BKL-002).
- Do not read `pointerType` anywhere except `input/PointerInput`.
- Do not add a UI to clear `stylusSeen` (the toggle covers the use case).
