# V2-M13 — Slides and zoom

**Tag at the end:** `v1.13.0`. **Model:** mid-tier, medium effort. **Prerequisite:** M5, M11 done.

**Requirements:** R-4b/c, R-60a…e, R-23a…f, R-39b/c, R-26a…d, R-17a…d, R-57a/b, D-18.

Goal: slide construction offers page-break modes, side areas show real content, zoom works like a standard PDF viewer in organization mode and follows the fingers in annotation mode, and slide changes can have a dead time.

## Steps (the owner commits after each)

### S1 — Aspect ratio and page-break control (R-4b/c, R-60, D-18)

1. Annotation mode bottom bar (right side): aspect-ratio decrease/increase with V1 steps (range 0.5–2.0, next multiple of 0.1 strictly below or above). The **start value** is the device full-screen ratio in the current orientation. The PDF area is the largest area for that ratio; the remaining strips are dark gray (tunables). On change a highlighted rectangle and the value show briefly. The V1 per-PDF override per orientation (SLD-015) stays (D-18); the start value rule replaces the V1 "presentation viewport" rule (SLD-014): the edit to those tests is explicitly allowed and listed.
2. Page-break control: **off** (V1 packing), **auto** (V1 packing plus a slide break after each page, optimal distribution of systems), **1 to 4 slides per page** (systems split by equal total height, whole systems only, `core/` with fixtures). For 1–4 the aspect-ratio setting is ignored, the full device screen is used, the ratio buttons are disabled; fixed breaks (slide breaks) and break hints are ignored. One setting for the whole app (Slide mode group, persisted through the registry).
3. Contracts and fixtures for the split in `core/` first (at least 20 cases: 1 system, many small systems, one tall system, 4 per page with uneven heights).

### S2 — Side fill (R-23a…f)

Left and right free areas are filled with the real PDF content beside the systems, **undimmed**; dimming above and below stays (V1 SLD-009 changes: no dimming of filler to the left and right of shown systems). Applies in annotation mode, slide mode and 1–4 modes. Gray only where the page has no content. Slide mode stays full screen without controls. The padding reduction idea (#24) is dropped. The affected V1 composition/dimming tests are explicitly allowed to change; list them.

### S3 — Zoom (R-39b/c, R-26a…d)

1. Organization mode bottom bar: page indicator and navigation plus standard viewer zoom: in/out, fit page, fit width, 100 %, other percentages. The zoom is restored on reload (native paid only, feature switch from M3).
2. Annotation mode: pinch zooms around the moving midpoint **and pans with it** (apply the midpoint movement as pan during the pinch; the content stays under the fingers); fix the pan clamp that shifts the view at low zoom. Ctrl+wheel and trackpad pinch around the pointer and the zoom buttons stay as V1 (NAV-013/014). Zoom returns to fit on every slide change (NAV-015). Slide mode: no zoom (NAV-017).
3. Test the pinch math in `core/gestures.ts` first (fixtures: two fingers moving apart and sideways, clamping at low zoom).

### S4 — Dead time and fullscreen (R-17, R-57)

1. Dead time in slide mode: one global setting in seconds (Slide mode group; default 0.5 s, min/max/step in tunables) applied to all triggers (keys, taps, swipes, wheel, pedals, buttons). Ignored triggers are dropped, not queued. No dead time outside slide mode. V1's auto-repeat and wheel locks stay.
2. Fullscreen on entering slide mode on desktop browsers stays unchanged (PLT-004, PLT-016); no new tests.

## Tests

- Unit: ratio start value, 1–4 splitting, auto distribution, side-fill composition (rectangles), pinch with pan, dead time gate (time-injected).
- E2E: ratio buttons and highlight, 1–4 modes on phone and tablet profiles, undimmed sides (pixel sampling on a corpus page), zoom controls and reload restore (paid config), pinch with synthetic pointers, dead time drops a second tap.
- Manual (owner, tablet): pinch feels right; side fill looks right on a real score.

## Do not

- Do not change the detection or the playing order.
- Do not add zoom in slide mode.
- Do not add per-app ratio (the per-PDF override stays).
- Do not touch the V1 wheel/auto-repeat locks.

- [ ] Organization zoom controls and restore; annotation pinch keeps content under the fingers.
